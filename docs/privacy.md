# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026_

This site is a static website. It has no accounts, no sign-up forms, and no comments. I do not collect or store your personal data.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on **GitHub Pages**. GitHub may log technical data, such as your IP address, when you visit, for security and to run the service. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement){ target="_blank" rel="noopener" }.

## Fonts

The site loads its fonts from **Google Fonts**. Your browser connects to Google's servers to download them, which shares your IP address with Google. See the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy){ target="_blank" rel="noopener" }.

## Cookies and analytics

This site does not use tracking cookies or analytics. Your light or dark mode choice is saved in your own browser's local storage and is never sent to me.

## External links

Articles link to other websites, such as fund providers and justETF. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

## Changes

If this policy changes, for example if I add analytics, I will update this page.
