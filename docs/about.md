# About Us

## Why this site exists

I'm a beginner from Sri Lanka learning how to invest in ETFs. Instead of keeping my notes private, I'm writing them in public, one step at a time.

The goal is simple:

- **For me:** clear notes I can come back to later.
- **For you:** a beginner-friendly guide, written from a Sri Lankan point of view.

## How I write

- I write each article after I learn the topic, so the guide grows step by step.
- Each article lists its sources.
- If I find a mistake, I fix it and keep the guide up to date.
